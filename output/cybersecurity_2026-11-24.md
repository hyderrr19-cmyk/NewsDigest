## CrowdStrike to acquire Zscaler for $44 billion

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** 2026-11-23T16:20:11+00:00

Cybersecurity giant CrowdStrike has agreed to buy Zscaler in a $43.9 billion all-stock deal, the companies announced Monday. Zscaler is a San Jose, California-based tech company that provides cloud cybersecurity products to about 8,000 enterprise companies. CrowdStrike, which secures the computers, laptops, phones, and other devices connected to a company’s network, said the deal will unite its endpoint protection software with Zscaler’s zero-trust platform to better defend their customers against data breaches.
